What you get for unused leave, and how much of it is tax-free.

What is the Leave Encashment Calculator?

When you leave a job you are paid for unused leave. Enter your last Basic + DA and the leave days to see the amount and the tax relief.

How it works

One day’s salary = monthly Basic + DA / 30. Amount = one day’s salary x leave days. For a private-sector employee, the tax-free part is the lowest of three amounts: the amount received, 10 months’ salary, or 25 lakh.

Frequently asked questions

When is tax charged on leave encashment?

While you are still employed, encashment is taxed like salary. On retirement or resignation, a private-sector employee gets relief up to the limit above. A government employee gets full relief.

How many leave days can be encashed in a year?

For the exemption, leave beyond 30 days per completed year of service is not counted. Your company’s own policy may differ.

Why divide by 30 for one day’s salary?

Income tax rules count a month as 30 days.
