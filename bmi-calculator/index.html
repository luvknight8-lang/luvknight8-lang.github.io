BMI Calculator

Height aur weight se Body Mass Index.

Calculator load ho raha hai. Agar ye dikhta rahe, to JavaScript chalu karein.

BMI Calculator kya hai

BMI (Body Mass Index) height ke hisaab se vajan ka ek aam napne ka tareeka hai. Height aur weight daalkar aap dekh sakte hain ki aapka BMI kis category me aata hai.

Ye kaise kaam karta hai

Formula: BMI = weight (kg) / (height in meter)^2. 18.5 se kam kam vajan, 18.5 se 24.9 normal, 25 se 29.9 zyada vajan aur 30 ya usse upar motapa mana jata hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawal

Kya BMI se sehat ka poora pata chalta hai?

Nahi. BMI ek mota andaza hai. Muscle, umar aur body type ka isme dhyan nahi rakha jata. Sehat ke baare me doctor se salah lein.

Kya BMI sabhi ke liye ek jaisa hota hai?

Ye ek general category hai. Bachchon aur kuch khaas halat me alag tareeke apnaye jaate hain.
