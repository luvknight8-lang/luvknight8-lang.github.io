What is Hisaab Kitab?

Hisaab Kitab is a website of free calculators and converters built to make money and everyday sums easier. Every calculator runs right in your browser, and the numbers you enter are not saved on any server.

The investing calculators (SIP, lumpsum, FD, RD, CAGR) estimate how your money can grow over time. The EMI calculator shows your monthly instalment before you take a loan. The GST calculator adds or removes GST on a bill. The salary tools cover gratuity, in-hand pay, income tax, EPF, HRA, leave encashment and bonus as per Indian rules. Converters turn height (cm and feet), length, weight, temperature and area from one unit to another.

Many tools follow Indian rules and use rupees and the lakh and crore system. Each page says which rules it uses.
