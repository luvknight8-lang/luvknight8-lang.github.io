Hisaab Kitab kya hai

Hisaab Kitab ek free calculators ki website hai, jo paise aur roz ke hisaab ko aasaan banane ke liye bani hai. Har calculator seedha aapke browser me chalta hai, aur aap jo bhi rashi daalte hain wo kisi server par save nahi hoti.

Nivesh ke calculators (SIP, lumpsum, FD, RD, CAGR) andaza dete hain ki aapka paisa samay ke saath kitna badh sakta hai. EMI calculator se loan lene se pehle mahine ki kisht jaan sakte hain. GST calculator se bill me GST jod ya hata sakte hain.

Ye calculators sirf andaza lagane ke liye hain. Asli rashi bank, company ya sarkari niyam ke hisaab se alag ho sakti hai. Ye nivesh, tax ya sehat ki salah nahi hai.
