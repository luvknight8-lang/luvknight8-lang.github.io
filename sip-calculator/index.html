SIP Calculator kya hai

SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) me aap har mahine ek tay rashi mutual fund me lagate hain. Is calculator se aap pehle hi andaza laga sakte hain ki mahine ke nivesh, expected return aur saalon ke hisaab se kitni rashi ban sakti hai.

Ye kaise kaam karta hai

Formula: M = P x ((1 + i)^n - 1) / i x (1 + i). Yahan P mahine ki rashi hai, i mahine ki dar (saal ki dar ko 12 se bhaag dekar) aur n kul mahine. Maana jata hai ki nivesh har mahine ki shuruaat me hota hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawal

Kya SIP me return guaranteed hota hai?

Nahi. Mutual fund ka return market ke hisaab se upar neeche hota rehta hai. Ye calculator sirf ek andaza deta hai, guarantee nahi.

SIP kam se kam kitne se shuru ho sakti hai?

Kai fund house chhoti rashi, jaise 100 ya 500 rupaye, se SIP shuru karne dete hain. Ye fund par depend karta hai.
