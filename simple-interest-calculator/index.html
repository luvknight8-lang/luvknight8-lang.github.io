Simple Interest Calculator kya hai

Simple interest ya sadharan byaj sirf mool rashi par lagta hai. Ye aam taur par chhote karz ya kuch tarah ke loan me use hota hai.

Calculator ke neeche ek table bhi dikhti hai, jisme saal ya mahine ke hisaab se rashi, byaj aur baaki rashi ka poora hisaab milta hai.

Ye kaise kaam karta hai

Formula: SI = P x R x T / 100. P mool rashi, R saal ki dar aur T saal hai. Kul rashi = P + SI.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawal

Simple interest kahan use hota hai?

Kai chhote loan, udhaar aur kuch tarah ke deposit me sadharan byaj ka hisaab hota hai.

Kya mahine ke hisaab se bhi nikal sakte hain?

Haan. Samay ko saal me badal lein, jaise 6 mahine ka matlab 0.5 saal.
